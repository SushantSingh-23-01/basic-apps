This frontend app is a responsive web-based music player built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features:
- **Dynamic Playlist Loading**
- **Playback Controls**
- **Interactive Seeking & Progress Tracking**
- **Auto-Play Next Track**
